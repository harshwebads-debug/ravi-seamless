PROCESS
Precision Engineering

The Art of
Seamless Creation.

At Ravi Seamless, manufacturing isn't just a process; it's a discipline. From the raw billet to the final finished pipe, every step is monitored by state-of-the-art technology and seasoned metallurgists. We control the molecular integrity of steel to deliver pipes that withstand the world's toughest environments, ensuring absolute reliability in every inch.

View Technical Specs
01

Sourcing

We procure high-grade steel billets exclusively from certified global vendors to ensure chemical purity and structural integrity.

02

Piercing

Solid billets are heated to 1250°C and pierced into hollow shells using rotary piercing technology with extreme precision.

03

Rolling

Precision mandrel rolling reduces wall thickness to achieve exact tolerances required by clients, ensuring uniform density.

04

Testing

Final dispatch requires 100% clearance in Hydrostatic, Ultrasonic, and Eddy Current tests to guarantee zero defects.
