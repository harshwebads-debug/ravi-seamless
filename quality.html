Advanced Testing

World-Class Laboratory

Equipped with the latest NDT and destructive testing machinery.

01

Hydrostatic Test

Every single pipe undergoes high-pressure water testing to ensure leak-proof performance under extreme conditions.

02

Ultrasonic Test (UT)

Advanced NDT method used to detect internal flaws and ensure uniform wall thickness across the entire length.

03

Chemical Analysis

Spectrometer testing verifies the exact chemical composition of the steel to match client specifications.

04

Mechanical Testing

Tensile, hardness, and impact tests are conducted to certify the physical strength and durability of the tubes.

05

Visual Inspection

100% visual and dimensional checks by certified inspectors to ensure perfect surface finish and straightness.

06

Certification

We provide EN 10204 3.1 & 3.2 Material Test Certificates (MTC) with every dispatch for complete traceability.
